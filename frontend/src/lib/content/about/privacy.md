## general terms

we believe privacy is important, so we don't collect, store, or sell any data related to you.

## analytics

we look at anonymous, aggregated numbers through [our own umami instance](https://analytics.wdkq.cc) it sets no cookies and builds no fingerprint, so a single visit cannot be traced back to you. 

if you would rather we counted nothing at all, you can switch it off in the settings at any time. 

## queries

your query travels from your browser to a server, and from here to the search engines you turned on. it is never written to disk and never handed to anyone else.

all queries are processed in RAM and sent to search engines in anonymized form, so searches cannot be traced back to anyone.

## self-hosted instances

we do our best to keep this instance private. anyone can run their own copy of clasp though, and we can neither see nor control what they do with it, so we cannot answer for it. read the source before you trust one.
